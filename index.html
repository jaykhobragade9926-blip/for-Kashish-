Chapter one

The story I still carry.

01

For Kashish

Wishing you a very happy birthday, Kashish. ❤️

You truly mean a lot to me, more than I could ever put into words.

02

The first time — Magnum

I still remember the first time I met you at Magnum. From the very first moment I saw you, there was something about you that I couldn’t explain. I just felt drawn towards you. I loved your nature, the way you behaved, the way you carried yourself… everything about you made me feel something I had never felt before. Maybe it really was love at first sight for me.

03

Being around you

From sitting together at Magnum to going on breaks together, I simply loved being around you. I don’t know what it was, but whenever I was with you, I felt genuinely happy. And within those days, I slowly realized that what I was feeling wasn’t just a simple attraction—I had actually fallen in love with you.

04

Trust

That’s why I gathered the courage to tell you how I felt. I know you probably didn’t trust me completely at that time because of the things you had been through in your past. Those experiences had given you reasons to have trust issues, and I completely understood that. But after all my efforts, when you finally started trusting me, even a little, I felt incredibly lucky.

I had promised you that I would never leave you, no matter what happened. And honestly, I never did.

05

The little moments

I still remember all those little moments we shared—the rides together on the bike, waiting for one or two hours just to get a glimpse of you, your smile, our first kiss, the way you would sometimes hold my hand, our silly little arguments, our endless debates and all those small moments that somehow became the most beautiful memories of my life.

Because of you, I even started waking up early in the morning. There are so many little things about me that changed because of you, and maybe you never even realized how much of an impact you had on my life.

06

Grateful

Even today, I have so many feelings inside me, but I can’t express them the way I used to. Maybe some things have changed, maybe life has taken us somewhere neither of us expected. But no matter what, I will always be grateful for everything you gave me—the memories, the happiness, the love, and the moments that I will carry with me for the rest of my life.

You have done something for me that I can never repay. You became a beautiful part of my life, and I feel truly blessed that, even for a little while, I got to experience that kind of happiness with you.

07

Today, on your birthday

I never knew those beautiful moments would be temporary. If I had known, I would have held on to every second a little tighter.

But today is your birthday, and more than anything, I just want you to be happy. I hope life gives you everything your heart wishes for. I hope you always keep that beautiful smile, and I hope you find all the love, peace and happiness you deserve.

Happy Birthday, Kashish. ❤️

No matter where life takes us, a part of my heart will always be grateful that I met you.
